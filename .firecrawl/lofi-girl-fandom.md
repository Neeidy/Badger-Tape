**Lofi Girl** (formerly known as **ChilledCow**) is a French music channel best known for its constant-running live streams that play lo-fi music promoted as being meant for studying, playing video games, or simply relaxing.

The channel's mascot is the Study Girl (commonly referred to as the "Lofi Girl"), an anime-styled girl created by Juan Pablo Machado.[\[1\]](https://lofigirl.fandom.com/wiki/Lofi_Girl_(YouTube_channel)#cite_note-1)[\[2\]](https://lofigirl.fandom.com/wiki/Lofi_Girl_(YouTube_channel)#cite_note-2) The character has become a meme and is often parodied, cosplayed, and redrawn by fans.

In May 2025, the channel celebrated 15 million subscribers.

### Introduction: The Genesis of a Digital Phenomenon\[ [Sign in to edit](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Flofigirl.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FLofi_Girl_%28YouTube_channel%29%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")\]

The Lofi Girl channel’s origins can be traced to a creative endeavor by French producer Dimitri Somoguy, who established the YouTube channel "ChilledCow" on 18 March 2015.1 The initial concept was straightforward: to provide a continuous, 24/7 stream of lo-fi hip hop music intended as a backdrop for listeners engaged in work or study.1 This idea was a response to the growing popularity of lo-fi music, a subgenre of downtempo music that had risen to prominence in the early 2010s.

Lo-fi, short for "low fidelity," is characterized by its intentional imperfections, such as vinyl crackle, tape hiss, and distorted audio.3 It is a genre that finds its roots in Japanese hip-hop, with producers like Nujabes mastering the art of blending steady, often looped beats with peaceful instrumentals and sonic "flaws".4 This aesthetic of raw authenticity and introspection resonated with a wide audience and set the stage for the channel's future success.3

To visually accompany the music, Dimitri initially used a looping GIF of the character Shizuku Tsukishima from the 1995 Studio Ghibli film _Whisper of the Heart_.1 The character, depicted studying diligently with headphones, perfectly captured the mood and purpose of the stream. This visual choice was a deliberate nod to the genre's Japanese origins and helped establish a cozy, focused ambiance. However, this early reliance on a copyrighted character would prove to be both a critical turning point and a foundational challenge for the channel.

### The Formative Years: 2015-2017\[ [Sign in to edit](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Flofigirl.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FLofi_Girl_%28YouTube_channel%29%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")\]

The official history of the channel begins in 2015. On 18 March of that year, Dimitri Somoguy created the YouTube channel "ChilledCow".1 For the first two years, the channel's presence was primarily focused on music curation, but its trajectory was fundamentally altered in 2017.

On 25 February 2017, the channel launched its first continuous, 24/7 live stream of lo-fi hip hop beats.1 This stream, featuring the now-familiar image of the studious anime girl, quickly gained a following. However, the use of the Studio Ghibli character carried an inherent risk. This came to a head between July and August 2017, when YouTube took down the live stream due to a copyright violation claim from the Japanese animation studio.1 The unauthorized use of the character Shizuku Tsukishima was a clear breach of copyright law, and the channel was forced to cease its broadcast.

This setback, rather than ending the channel, became a crucial catalyst for its future. The forced removal of the copyrighted image meant that the channel had to create its own original character to maintain its aesthetic and re-launch the stream. This strategic pivot transformed the brand from a generic music stream using popular imagery to a unique intellectual property with a distinct, recognizable icon. Dimitri put out a public call for artists, seeking a new character that would maintain the "Ghibli-esque" aesthetic.1 By September 2017, the stream was back online with a temporary, custom animation of a girl studying, setting the stage for the creation of the character who would soon become a global icon.1

### The Emergence of a Digital Icon: 2018-2020\[ [Sign in to edit](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Flofigirl.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FLofi_Girl_%28YouTube_channel%29%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D5&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")\]

The pivotal year for the channel's visual identity was 2018. In March, the iconic Lofi Girl character, officially named Jade, made her debut, replacing the temporary animation.2 The character was brought to life by Colombian-French artist Juan Pablo Machado, who responded to the open call for a "student busy revising for her classes, with Miyazaki-esque visuals".2 Machado's design incorporated a real-world element, placing the La Croix-Rousse neighborhood of Lyon, France, in the cityscape visible through the window.2 The new character's animation was clean, minimalist, and perfectly captured the essence of the stream. With this new visual identity, the channel formally launched its main live stream, titled "Lofi hip hop mix - Beats to Relax/Study to," in March.9

The channel continued to grow steadily, but in 2020, it faced a new challenge. In February, YouTube mistakenly terminated the channel, citing a violation of its Terms of Service.10 This incident, though less publicized than the copyright disputes, was an early indicator of the systemic flaws in YouTube's automated moderation system. The channel was quickly reinstated after YouTube acknowledged the error.10 This event was an early signal of the channel's vulnerability to automated platform policies, a problem that would resurface and become a defining part of its narrative. As the world entered global lockdowns due to the COVID-19 pandemic, the channel's significance deepened. Lofi Girl became a "digital sanctuary" and a "safe companion" for millions of students and remote workers who were isolated at home.6 The looped animation and calming beats offered a sense of "body doubling," providing a non-intrusive form of companionship that helped people stay focused and feel less alone.6

### Strategic Rebranding and Global Acclaim: 2021-2022\[ [Sign in to edit](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Flofigirl.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FLofi_Girl_%28YouTube_channel%29%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D6&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")\]

The year 2021 marked a pivotal moment in the channel's brand strategy. On 18 March, six years after its creation, an official announcement was made: the channel would be rebranded from "ChilledCow" to "Lofi Girl".1 The decision, explained in community posts, was based on the recognition that the Lofi Girl character had become the true icon and identity of the channel.2 This strategic move was a masterstroke in brand iconography, as it fully cemented the character's central role and positioned the brand to capitalize on its most recognizable asset. By renaming the channel after the character, the brand was transformed from a content aggregator to a powerful, licensable intellectual property, setting the stage for future collaborations and merchandising that would be built around the character's fame.

This strategic direction proved effective, as the channel continued to achieve major milestones. On 2 February 2022, the Lofi Girl channel reached a significant milestone of 10 million subscribers.1 The stream that was live at the time of the celebration had been running since 22 February 2020 and had accumulated over 7 million likes.1 However, a major crisis emerged just a few months later. In July 2022, the channel's two most popular livestreams, "lofi hip hop radio - beats to relax/study to" and "beats to sleep/chill to," were taken down by YouTube following a series of fraudulent copyright claims filed by a Malaysian company, "FMC Music Sdn Bhd Malaysia".7

The takedown sparked a massive outcry from the community, with fans expressing their dismay and using the hashtag #bringbacklofigirl to demand action.10 This event, which garnered international media attention, became a public flashpoint for a persistent problem on YouTube: the abuse of its automated copyright strike system.10 Within 48 hours, YouTube admitted that the claims were "abusive," terminated the claimant's account, and reinstated the streams, issuing a public apology.7 The Lofi Girl channel, using its immense platform, turned the incident into a form of creator advocacy, publicly urging YouTube to implement better protections and a more robust vetting process for claims, a problem that disproportionately affects smaller creators.10 The incident demonstrated the unequal power dynamics on the platform, where smaller creators often lack the visibility to have their cases resolved and face disincentives to file a counter-notice due to the legal requirements of providing personal information and agreeing to court jurisdiction.15

### Analysis of Core Pillars: Character, Community, and Commerce\[ [Sign in to edit](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Flofigirl.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FLofi_Girl_%28YouTube_channel%29%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D9&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")\]

#### The Business of Lo-fi: From Hobby to Label\[ [Sign in to edit](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Flofigirl.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FLofi_Girl_%28YouTube_channel%29%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D10&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")\]

The Lofi Girl channel's success is not solely a matter of content creation; it is a sophisticated business operation. What began as a passion project has evolved into a formidable music enterprise. The in-house label, Lofi Records, was founded in 2019 to manage the music featured on the channel.19 As the channel's popularity surged, particularly during the pandemic, the business faced the complex challenge of managing large volumes of daily sales and streaming data.20

To address this, Lofi Records partnered with LabelWorx, a distribution and royalty accounting solution.20 This partnership allowed Lofi Records to streamline its operations and focus on A&R (Artists and Repertoire) and other brand commitments.20 A key component of this business model is the commitment to providing monthly accounting to its artists, a professional standard that has helped the label expand its presence.20 The diversification of the business model from simple YouTube ad monetization to a multi-channel enterprise is a critical factor in the brand's long-term sustainability. By establishing Lofi Records, the brand created a new revenue stream from streaming services like Spotify and Apple Music, while also capitalizing on merchandise and physical products, such as a wide variety of themed vinyl record albums.18 This move insulated the brand from potential future changes in YouTube's policies and ensured a robust, multi-channel business.

#### The Power of the Character: Art, Psychology, and Community\[ [Sign in to edit](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Flofigirl.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FLofi_Girl_%28YouTube_channel%29%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D11&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")\]

At the heart of the Lofi Girl phenomenon is the character herself. Her appeal lies in her role as an ideal "study buddy" or "safe companion," a concept that can be analyzed through the lens of parasocial relationships.2 The character provides a non-intrusive companion for solitary activities, and her consistent presence, hunched over her desk with headphones, creates a sense of "body doubling" that helps listeners feel connected and focused.6 The deliberate vagueness of her backstory and the lack of a detailed, pre-written narrative encourages audience projection and co-creation of "lore".6

The channel's success is also deeply tied to the psychological and cognitive benefits of lo-fi music. The genre's slow, gentle tempo has been shown to reduce cortisol levels and induce feelings of relaxation, which can help alleviate stress and anxiety.12 Research suggests that listening to this type of music can even slow a person's heart rate, helping them to focus on the task at hand.12 The looped nature of the beats provides a sense of predictability that helps keep listeners "in the zone," while gentle ambient sounds like rain or a vinyl crackle act as a buffer, drowning out jarring noises and creating a stress-free environment.12 Studies have shown that background music can improve working memory and cognitive performance, with one 2023 study finding that students who listened to lo-fi or classical music performed significantly better on a comprehension and math assessment than those who took the test in silence.12 The lack of lyrics in lo-fi music also makes it less distracting than other genres.21

It is important to clarify a common piece of misinformation found in certain sources, which incorrectly state that the character was created by the YouTuber and artist Juan Pablo Machado, and that the channel's origin can be traced back to a 2013 college project.22 The channel was created by Dimitri Somoguy in 2015, and the Lofi Girl character was designed by Juan Pablo Machado as a commissioned work in 2018.2 The widespread nature of this misinformation highlights the challenge of maintaining an accurate historical record for a brand with such a global and decentralized community.

### Conclusion and Outlook\[ [Sign in to edit](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Flofigirl.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FLofi_Girl_%28YouTube_channel%29%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D12&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")\]

The history of the Lofi Girl channel is a comprehensive case study in the evolution of digital content and brand building. Its journey, from a niche hobby to a sophisticated media company, was propelled by a series of strategic decisions and a profound connection with its audience. The initial copyright strike from Studio Ghibli, a challenge that could have ended the channel, instead became the catalyst for creating an iconic, ownable character. This intellectual property was then cemented by the deliberate rebranding from "ChilledCow" to "Lofi Girl," a move that fully aligned the brand's identity with its most powerful asset.

The channel’s success is also a testament to its ability to leverage its community. The public outcry following the 2022 fraudulent copyright strike not only restored the streams but also brought critical attention to systemic issues in platform policy. In this instance, Lofi Girl became an inadvertent advocate for creator rights, demonstrating the influence a large, engaged community can wield.

Looking forward, the Lofi Girl brand is poised for continued growth. The expansion into a "Lofi Universe" with characters like Synthwave Boy suggests a long-term strategy of deepening the brand’s lore and exploring new music genres and aesthetics. The increasing number of high-profile collaborations with major corporations indicates that the brand's value is no longer limited to its YouTube presence but is a powerful tool for marketing and cultural resonance across diverse industries. The channel’s history demonstrates that a simple, consistent concept, meticulously nurtured and built upon, can become a cultural phenomenon that transcends its original medium, influencing everything from study habits and mental well-being to brand partnerships and internet culture.
